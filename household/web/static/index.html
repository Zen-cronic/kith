What you're agreeing to

A computer program will read your letter. This app does not save letters or transcripts to a file. Ending the visit clears this screen. You can print a summary and an approved reply. Legal notices require help from a person. You can choose not to use the program.

Stored after the visit
This app keeps no saved letter or transcript. Any external model provider has its own retention policy.
Where the reading happens
This computer, in demo mode.
What you take home
A printable summary and, when approved, a reply to review with staff.
What we will not do
Draft your answer to an eviction notice, an immigration letter, or a court document. A person does that with you.
